# Campus 2035 branding

## Mark
An open book (learning) under a four-point spark (the next decade) on a deep-teal squircle.
Source: `src/components/ui/Logo.tsx` (`LogoMark`, `Logo`) and the favicon `src/app/icon.svg`.

| Part | Value |
|---|---|
| Tile gradient | `#14A394` to `#0B5D57` (top-left to bottom-right), corner radius 28% |
| Book | `#FFFFFF`, spine `#0F766E` |
| Spark | `#FFD66B` |
| Clear space | At least 25% of the mark's width on every side |
| Minimum size | 20 px (use the mark alone below 120 px wide) |

## Wordmark
"Campus" in the heading font (Bold), "2035" in `#0F766E`. Tagline "Smart classroom OS" in small caps, only at 30 px mark size and above.

## Colour
Light theme only. Primary `#0F766E`, text `#12272A`, surfaces white and pale mint. Accents (pink, amber, mint, teal) are used on dashboard tiles only.

## Mascot
Nova, the teal robot (`src/components/ui/Mascot.tsx`), is the friendly face for empty states, login and the Copilot. It never replaces the logo.

## Do not
- Recolour the tile, rotate the mark, or add shadows or outlines.
- Place the mark on a busy photo; use the white wordmark on a solid teal field instead.
- Use the spark on its own as the logo.

## Voice
Plain, warm and short. Say what happened and what to do next. No jargon, no exclamation marks in errors.
