Brand board · v2

Campus 2035

For schools, Campus 2035 is the smart-classroom OS that turns a student's ID card into their whole school day.

Logo

A bold “C” ring for Campus; the amber square in its opening is one cell of a Smart ID code. Rationale: the card is the product's front door, so the mark shows the door being opened by a single cell.

Wordmark
Campus 2035
Clear space
At least the height of the amber square (¼ of the mark) on all sides.
Don't
  • Recolour the ring or square
  • Rotate, outline or add shadows
  • Use the square alone as the logo
